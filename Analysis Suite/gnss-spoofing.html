How to read it

      
A cell does not move. For every LTE cell the scan finds where most of its devices are (its home), then keeps the samples that claim to be far from it. Many homes pointing at one spot — often an airport — is the signature of spoofing.


      Not covered: jamming (the position is lost, not moved), and regions where spoofing is so constant that the cell's home itself is wrong. Displacements under ~30 km are below the 0.1° grid. A finding is a lead, not proof.
